About Wird

Version 0.1

Wird helps you keep a daily portion of the Qur'an. Your days, tracks and recordings stay on this phone.

Qur'an text and translations

From Quran.com, bundled in the app so they open offline.

English: Saheeh International. Hausa: Abubakar Mahmoud Gumi. Mushaf pages download from Quran.com when you first open them.

Theme

Header photo

By the clock: the day photo until Maghrib, then sunset. The app takes Maghrib from its own prayer times; here it switches at 18:00. Dark mode always uses the sunset photo, dimmed.

Tracks

Your phone has one track, School Quran. Hifz and Weekend Review are examples so you can see a longer list.

First-time experience

Your answers build the track that Home shows afterwards. Start over brings your real data back.

What's real

  • Your track, page 3, the five days and the one recited day come from your phone.
  • Sūrah names, juz' pages and every verse come from the app's bundled files, not typed by hand.
  • A grey message at the bottom means that screen already exists and keeps working as it does now.
