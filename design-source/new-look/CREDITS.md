## Text

The prototype reads all sūrah names, juz' pages and verse text from `prototype/data.js`. That file
was generated from the app's own `SurahIndex.kt`, `assets/arabic` and `assets/translations/20`
(Saheeh International). The Arabic sūrah names come from Quran.com's chapter list. None of it
was typed by hand.
