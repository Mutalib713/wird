Welcome to Wird

A little Qur'an, every day.

What is a wird (وِرْد)?

Your wird is the portion of Qur'an you promise to read each day. It can be two pages, one page or a few verses. Small and steady is the point.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ١ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ٢ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ٣ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ٤ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ٥

Miss a day and your streak starts again. Your total days read never goes down.

Recite & Review

Say your portion out loud.

How Recite & Review works

Read aloud from the page or from memory, at home or on a trotro. Wird listens on your phone and marks any verse that may need another look.

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ١ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ٢ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ٣

May need another look

It works offline after a one-time download: 42 MB, or 78 MB for the more accurate model. Wird never uploads your voice.

Read it another way? Tap Mark done. Both count, and your history keeps them apart.

Four things need Mutalib's yes before the build

  1. Quick start begins at An‑Nās and works back. Today it begins at An‑Naba' and walks away from Juz' ʿAmma, so sūrahs 79 to 114 never come up.
  2. "Which way" and "where to start" share one screen, because the way decides where the start is.
  3. The splash button says "See how Wird works", so it names where it goes. The reference's button used a generic label.
  4. The phone's own fonts: Noto Serif and Noto Naskh Arabic. The mockups used Literata and Amiri, which the app doesn't have.

Sample data

Amina, the streak numbers and the three example tracks are made up for the picture. Every Arabic line and translation is copied by a script from the app's own files.

Fixed on the way

  • The gateway promised "100% offline… never consumes data". Pages, audio, the review model and tafsir all use data, so it now says which.
  • The name step showed readers "Sacred Rule 1", an internal note.
  • "Takes under 45 seconds" was never measured. Gone.
  • The Bismillah translation added two phrases the Arabic above it doesn't say. It now translates only what is shown.
  • The Yā‑Sīn shortcut opened page 442, two pages into the sūrah. Yā‑Sīn starts on page 440.
  • The splash hadith is quoted word for word, with its number.
  • No emoji on buttons: the lightning bolt on Quick start is gone (Sacred Rule 6).
  • Notifications are asked after the first finished day, not at the end of setup (Mutalib's call, 3 Oct).

Shape and space

  • Cards: 16 from the screen edge, radius 24, padding 20, a 1 dp rule edge, shadow 0 6 18 at deep 8%.
  • Tiles radius 14; inputs and options 12 to 16; buttons 56 tall and fully round.
  • Every tap target at least 48 dp. 12 between items in a card, 8 between chips.
  • Intro band 276 tall (31%), setup band 220. Card starts 14 below the band.
  • Band sky: sky-top 0%, sky-upper 28%, sky-glow 70%, plus a cream radial glow at 60% across, 78% down.
  • Splash, measured from Mutalib's reference: icon top 33.6% and 15.1% tall; wordmark 50.5%; tagline 59.4%; hadith 64.5%; button 79.7%, 64 tall, 34 from each side.
  • Review mark: the app's own review token, #D9A441 at 25%.
  • Leaf on the wordmark: #7EBB6A on dark sky, as Home draws it; green on the light splash.
Your first day is done

Want a reminder tomorrow?

Wird can send one notification a day for Daily Reading, about 30 minutes after Maghrib, with that day's portion in it. Nothing else.

To work out when Maghrib is, Wird asks for your rough location once. Without it, the reminder comes at 8:00 pm.
