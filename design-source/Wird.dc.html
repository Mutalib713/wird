My pick: one stroke, one dot
A single elongated stroke — the kashida that stretches a word to fill a justified mushaf line — with one dot beneath it. It is drawn from the page rather than from religious iconography, so it does not compete with Muslim Pro's crescent or Tarteel's waveform. It survives 48px, and it reads at a glance as a line of writing and nothing else.
